How to use this TI 84 calculator online

The TI-84 family from Texas Instruments is the graphing calculator most US students meet in algebra, pre-calculus, statistics and calculus. This online TI 84 calculator copies the parts you use every day, the home screen and the graphing keys, so you can practise the same keystrokes without the handheld in front of you.

The keys that matter most

Y=

Type the functions you want to graph: Y1 to Y4. Use X,T,θ,n for the variable X.

WINDOW

Set Xmin, Xmax, Ymin, Ymax and the tick spacing (Xscl, Yscl).

ZOOM

Quick windows: ZStandard, ZoomIn, ZoomOut, ZDecimal and ZTrig.

TRACE

Move along a curve with the arrow keys and read X and Y.

GRAPH

Draw every function that has an expression in Y=.

2nd

Uses the blue label above a key: √, π, sin⁻¹, 10ˣ, eˣ, CALC, TABLE, QUIT and more.

MODE

Switches between RADIAN and DEGREE for trigonometry.

2nd + TRACE

Opens CALC: value at X, zero and intersect.

Getting started in 5 steps

  1. Type a calculation on the home screen, for example 2^10, and press ENTER. The result is stored in Ans.
  2. Press Y= and enter a function such as X²−X−6 in Y1.
  3. Press GRAPH. If you see nothing, press ZOOM and choose ZStandard.
  4. Press TRACE and use ◀ ▶ (or click the graph) to read points on the curve.
  5. Press 2nd TRACE to open CALC and find zeros or intersections.

Worked examples

1 · Solve a quadratic

Enter Y1 = X²−X−6, graph it, then choose CALC → zero. The calculator finds X = −2 and X = 3, the two solutions of x² − x − 6 = 0.

2 · Where two graphs meet

Enter Y1 = X² and Y2 = 2X+1. CALC → intersect returns X ≈ −0.414 and X ≈ 2.414, which are 1 − √2 and 1 + √2.

3 · Trig in degrees

Press MODE to switch to DEGREE, then type sin(30). The answer is 0.5. In RADIAN mode the same keys give −0.988, a classic exam mistake.

4 · A table of values

With Y1 = 2X+3, press 2nd GRAPH for TABLE. Change TblStart and ΔTbl at the top to list the values you need.

TI-84 Plus, TI-84 Plus CE and TI-83 Plus

The TI-83 Plus (1999) set the standard for classroom graphing. The TI-84 Plus (2004) kept the same key layout and added more memory and a USB port. The TI-84 Plus CE (2015) is the version most students buy today: it is thinner, has a rechargeable battery and a backlit colour screen of 320 × 240 pixels. Because the keys are almost identical, what you learn on one model carries over to the others, and to this online TI 84 calculator.

Can you use a TI-84 on tests?

The handheld TI-84 Plus family is accepted on the calculator sections of major tests such as the SAT, the ACT and AP math and science exams, but always check the current rules for your exam. Online calculators like this one are not allowed in test rooms; use this page for homework, revision and to learn the keystrokes before exam day.

When a scientific calculator is enough

Not every class needs graphing. If your course or exam only allows scientific calculators, try the TI-30XS calculator, which has a four-line MultiView display with stacked fractions, or the classic one-line TI-30XA calculator with its a b/c fraction key and Σ+ statistics. Both are great for fractions, percentages, trigonometry and quick statistics.

Troubleshooting

  • The graph is empty. Your window may not show the curve. Use ZOOM → ZStandard, or check Ymin and Ymax.
  • ERR:SYNTAX. Look for a missing parenthesis, two operators in a row, or a subtraction key used where you meant (−).
  • Wrong trig answers. Check the status bar: DEGREE or RADIAN must match your problem.
  • ERR:DOMAIN. You asked for something undefined, such as √(−4) or log(0) in real mode.

TI 84 calculator FAQ

Is this TI 84 calculator free?

Yes. This TI 84 calculator runs free in your browser on computers, Chromebooks, tablets and phones, with no download or account.

Can I graph functions like on a real TI-84?

Yes. Enter up to four functions in Y=, set the WINDOW or use ZOOM, then press GRAPH. TRACE lets you move along a curve, and CALC finds values, zeros, intersections, minimums and maximums.

How do I find the zeros of a function?

Enter the function in Y1, press GRAPH, then 2nd and TRACE to open CALC and choose zero. The calculator lists every zero it finds inside the current window.

How do I switch between degrees and radians?

Press MODE. The status bar at the top of the screen shows DEGREE or RADIAN. Use DEGREE for most geometry problems and RADIAN for calculus and unit-circle work.

Can I use an online TI-84 calculator on the SAT or ACT?

No. Online calculators are not allowed in test rooms. The handheld TI-84 Plus family is accepted on the SAT, ACT and AP exams, so use this page to practise the keystrokes before test day.

What is the difference between the TI-84 Plus and the TI-84 Plus CE?

The TI-84 Plus CE, released in 2015, is thinner, has a backlit colour screen of 320 × 240 pixels and a rechargeable battery. The TI-84 Plus from 2004 has a monochrome screen and uses AAA batteries. The keys are almost the same.

Why does my graph not show up?

Usually the window does not include the curve. Press ZOOM and choose ZStandard, or check that Xmin is less than Xmax and Ymin is less than Ymax. Also check Y= for a syntax error.

Does this calculator have statistics and matrices?

This page focuses on the home screen, graphing and tables. For statistics, regressions, matrices and probability distributions, use the full TI 84 calculator on our main site.
